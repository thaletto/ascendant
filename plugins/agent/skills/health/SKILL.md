# Health and wellbeing guidance

Before applying this skill, read [`../AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md).

Use this skill when a user asks about energy, stress, recovery, recurring weaknesses, or health-related timing.

## What to review

1. Review the 1st house for general vitality and physical resilience.
2. Review the 6th house for daily strain, habits, and manageable health concerns.
3. Review the 8th house for deeper changes, recovery, and the need for extra care.
4. Note the Sun, Moon, Mars, Saturn, and the rulers of these houses.
5. Check planetary periods and movements for times when rest or support may be especially useful.

## Safety and tone

- Describe tendencies and periods of sensitivity, never a medical diagnosis.
- Encourage professional medical care for symptoms, emergencies, or treatment decisions.
- Use calm, practical language and avoid frightening predictions.
- Suggest ordinary supportive habits such as sleep, movement, food, rest, and medical follow-up.

## Output

Use these sections:

- **General vitality**
- **Areas needing care**
- **Current wellbeing phase**
- **Supportive routines**
