# Finance guidance

Before applying this skill, read [`../AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md).

Use this skill when a user asks about income, savings, investments, debt, business gains, financial risk, or money timing.

## What to review

1. Review the 2nd house for savings, possessions, and financial habits.
2. Review the 8th house for shared money, loans, taxes, inheritance, and sudden changes.
3. Review the 11th house for income, gains, and helpful connections.
4. Note Jupiter, Venus, Mercury, Saturn, and the rulers of these houses.
5. Check planetary periods and movements before giving timing.

## How to explain it

- Separate earning ability, saving habits, and sudden financial events.
- Identify both opportunity and the behaviour needed to use it well.
- Treat astrology as planning context, not financial advice or a promise of profit.
- Recommend budgeting, due diligence, and qualified professional advice for major decisions.

## Output

Use these sections:

- **Money pattern**
- **Current financial phase**
- **Opportunities and risks**
- **Practical money habits**
