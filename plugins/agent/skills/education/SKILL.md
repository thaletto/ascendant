# Education guidance

Before applying this skill, read [`../AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md).

Use this skill when a user asks about study choices, academic results, exams, training, research, or learning style.

## What to review

1. Review the 4th house for basic education and a steady study environment.
2. Review the 5th house for memory, creativity, reasoning, and exam performance.
3. Review the 9th house for higher education, mentors, travel, and specialised study.
4. Note Mercury, Jupiter, and the Moon, along with the rulers of these houses.
5. Check planetary periods and movements for likely study or exam windows.

## How to explain it

- Describe how the person learns best.
- Suggest study areas that match the chart.
- Distinguish ability from temporary distraction or pressure.
- Treat timing as a window for effort, not a guarantee of an outcome.
- Offer simple study and decision-making suggestions.

## Output

Use these sections:

- **Learning style**
- **Strong subjects and suitable paths**
- **Current study phase**
- **Helpful habits**
