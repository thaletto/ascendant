# Career guidance

Before applying this skill, read [`../AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md).

Use this skill when a user asks about work, a job change, promotion, business direction, workplace challenges, or professional timing.

## What to review

1. Look at the 6th house for daily work, duties, and competition.
2. Look at the 10th house for career direction, responsibility, and public standing.
3. Look at the 11th house for income from work, helpful networks, and gains.
4. Note the planets in these houses, the signs on them, and the strength of their rulers.
5. Check the current planetary period and major movements that may activate these houses.

## How to explain it

- Start with the user's natural strengths and practical opportunities.
- Separate long-term direction from short-term changes.
- Explain delays or pressure in everyday language.
- Give clear time windows only when the chart data supports them.
- End with practical actions the user can take.

## Output

Use these sections:

- **Career pattern**
- **Current phase**
- **Opportunities and challenges**
- **Practical next steps**
