question.

## Feelings

The charts may support an astrological inference about feelings only when both
records are present. Require at least one primary factor and one independent
corroborating factor, cite both, state conflicting evidence, and use
`tentative` or `moderate` confidence. Phrase the result as a chart-indicated
possibility, never verified fact. State that actual feelings require direct
confirmation. A chart cannot establish consent, availability, or relationship
status.

Do not collapse the comparison into a numeric compatibility score.
