# Family hierarchy

Required saved evidence: D1, dasha, present yogas, SAV, and provenance. No
divisional chart is required. Load transit positions only for a time-bound
question.

Select the primary factor from the relationship actually asked about:

| Role | Rule | Primary factor | Authority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mother | `PR-FAM-H04` | Fourth house and its lord | `BPHS-11.5`, `BPHS-11.14-16` |

| Household | `PR-FAM-HOUSEHOLD` | H4 primary; H3, H5, and H9 corroborating | Ascendant methodology |

| Rule | Factor | Layer | Rank | Authority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `PR-FAM-YOGA` | Present yoga that explicitly involves the selected house or lord | Natal promise | Modifying | Ascendant methodology |
| `PR-FAM-DASHA` | Active MD/AD lord of the selected relationship house | Vimshottari activation | Primary | `BPHS-46.3,14`; Ascendant methodology |

| `PR-FAM-SAV` | SAV score for the sign holding the selected natal house | SAV corroboration | Background | `BPHS-72.1-7`; Ascendant methodology |

state from one person's chart.
