# Family guidance

Before applying this skill, read [`../AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md).

Use this skill when a user asks about a family member or a family relationship.

## Choose the right chart

1. If the family member's birth details are available, use that person's chart.
2. If they are not available, clearly say that the reading is based on the user's chart and family houses only.
3. Never present an indirect reading as a complete reading of the other person.

## What to review

- Use the 4th house for home, mother, and emotional security.
- Use the 3rd house for siblings, communication, and shared effort.
- Use the 5th house for children, care, and creative connection.
- Use the 9th house for father, mentors, and family values.
- Consider the Moon, Jupiter, Sun, and the active planetary period.

## Output

Use these sections:

- **Relationship picture**
- **What is supportive**
- **Where care is needed**
- **Helpful communication or support**
