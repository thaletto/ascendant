# Relationship compatibility

Before applying this skill, read [`../AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md).

Use this skill only when two complete sets of birth details or two complete charts are available.

## What to compare

1. Compare the Moon signs and birth stars for emotional understanding.
2. Compare the Ascendants, Suns, and Moons for shared direction and everyday comfort.
3. Review each person's 5th, 7th, and 8th houses for affection, commitment, trust, and shared responsibilities.
4. Compare Venus and Mars for attraction and the way each person expresses desire.
5. Compare current planetary periods to see whether the partners' timing supports or strains each other.

## How to explain it

- Begin with natural points of connection.
- Describe differences as areas for communication, not verdicts.
- Call out serious concerns plainly but avoid dramatic or fatalistic language.
- Do not reduce compatibility to one score.
- Encourage consent, honest conversation, and professional help when needed.

## Output

Use these sections:

- **What brings you together**
- **How you may differ**
- **Current relationship phase**
- **Ways to support the relationship**
