## Feelings

The charts may support an astrological inference about feelings only when both
records are present. Require at least one primary factor and one independent
corroborating factor, cite both, state conflicting evidence, and use
`tentative` or `moderate` confidence. Phrase the result as a chart-indicated
possibility, never verified fact. State that actual feelings require direct
confirmation. A chart cannot establish consent, availability, or relationship
status.

Do not collapse the comparison into a numeric compatibility score.

## Cross-chart house-lord overlays

For each person, derive the lord of every selected house from that person's
own chart, then locate that same planet in the other person's chart. Record
the other person's house from their Lagna, sign, dignity, and occupants. Apply
the comparison in both directions; do not treat one person's overlay as
evidence for the reverse direction. Use D1 for the natal overlay and repeat it
in D9 when the topic requires D9. For a marriage comparison, the selected
houses are H7, H5, and H8. These overlays corroborate the individual natal
house/lord judgments; they do not replace them or establish feelings, consent,
availability, or relationship status.
