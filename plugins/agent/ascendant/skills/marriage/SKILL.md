# Marriage and partnership guidance

Before applying this skill, read [`../AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md).

Use this skill for questions about marriage, long-term partnership, commitment, relationship stability, or relationship timing.

## What to review

1. Review the 7th house for partnership and commitment.
2. Review the 5th house for affection, romance, and children.
3. Review the 8th house for trust, shared responsibilities, and joint finances.
4. Review the 4th house for home life and emotional comfort.
5. Note Venus, Jupiter, the Moon, Mars, Saturn, and the active planetary period.

## How to explain it

- Describe the person's relationship needs and patterns.
- Name supportive qualities before discussing strain.
- Explain disagreements as behaviours or circumstances, not fixed labels.
- For timing, give a window and the reason it is active.
- Encourage honest communication and consent; do not present astrology as a substitute for relationship or legal advice.

## Output

Use these sections:

- **Relationship pattern**
- **Current phase**
- **Strengths and pressure points**
- **Practical guidance**
