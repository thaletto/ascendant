# Property and vehicle guidance

Before applying this skill, read [`../AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md).

Use this skill when a user asks about buying, selling, building, renting, repairing, or financing a home, land, or vehicle.

## What to review

1. Review the 4th house for home, land, comfort, and vehicles.
2. Review the 11th house for gains and help from networks.
3. Review the 12th house for expenses, loans, and ongoing costs.
4. Note Mars, Venus, Saturn, and the rulers of these houses.
5. Check planetary periods and movements before giving a timing window.

## How to explain it

- Separate the desire for an asset from the ability to pay for it.
- Highlight paperwork, maintenance, delays, and hidden costs when the chart suggests care is needed.
- Encourage inspections, written agreements, legal review, and budget checks.
- Treat timing as a planning window, not a guarantee.

## Output

Use these sections:

- **Property picture**
- **Current buying or selling phase**
- **Costs and cautions**
- **Practical checklist**
